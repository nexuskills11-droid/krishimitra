Our Story

Coffee is not
just a beverage.

Third Wave Coffee was born from an obsession with craft. We believe that every bean carries the story of the land it grew in, the hands that harvested it, and the passion of the roaster who coaxed its potential.

We work exclusively with single-origin farms, practicing direct trade so farmers receive fair value. Each roast is small-batch, each brew deliberate. This is not fast food coffee — this is coffee as ceremony.
